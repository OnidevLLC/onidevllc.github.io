Privacy Policy

Effective: May 6, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Onidev LLC handles information in connection with our games, apps, websites, and related services.

2. Who We Are

Onidev LLC is an independent software company based in Maryland, USA. We develop and publish mobile games and apps for the Apple App Store and Google Play.

3. Information We Do Not Directly Collect

We do not require you to create an account with a username and password. Authentication is handled through Apple, Google, or Unity platform services.

We do not directly collect:

  • Account passwords
  • Real names
  • Mailing addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Precise location data
  • Contacts
  • Photos
  • Health data
  • Government-issued IDs

4. Information Processed to Operate Our Games and Apps

To provide our games and apps, we and our service providers may process the following categories of information:

Player Identifiers

Unity player IDs, platform account identifiers (such as Apple or Google account identifiers), and service-generated identifiers used to associate your game data with your account.

In-Game Profile Information

Display name, Unity-assigned username, avatar or customization choices, preferences, settings, and game progress.

Gameplay Data

Scores, match history, matchmaking data, leaderboard data, achievements, game state, cloud save data, and in-game economy balances.

Purchase and Entitlement Information

Records of purchases made through Apple or Google, used to provide, restore, validate, and support your purchases and entitlements.

Device and Technical Data

Device type, operating system, app version, region or country, diagnostics, and crash logs (if enabled).

Advertising and Analytics Data

Ad interactions, performance metrics, gameplay event analytics, and advertising or analytics identifiers.

5. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Operate game features such as sign-in, cloud save, friends, matchmaking, leaderboards, and multiplayer
  • Save and restore your game progress across devices
  • Provide, restore, and validate purchases
  • Manage in-game economies and entitlements
  • Understand how players use features so we can improve our games
  • Show ads to support free-to-play games
  • Diagnose crashes and technical issues
  • Comply with legal obligations

6. Service Providers

We use the following service providers to operate our games and apps:

  • Unity Gaming Services — Authentication, Friends, Cloud Save, Cloud Code, Leaderboards, and matchmaking
  • Unity LevelPlay — Advertising and ad mediation
  • Apple and Google — Platform services including app distribution, purchases, and account authentication
  • An analytics provider — Gameplay analytics and performance metrics
  • A crash reporting provider — Crash logs and diagnostics

These providers may process data according to their own documentation and privacy policies.

7. Advertising

Our free-to-play games may show ads through Unity LevelPlay and its ad mediation partners. Advertising partners may process device identifiers, ad interactions, and approximate location to serve and measure ads.

On iOS, we follow Apple's App Tracking Transparency rules. You may be asked for permission before certain advertising identifiers are used.

On Android, advertising identifiers and consent mechanisms are used in accordance with Google's policies.

8. Analytics and Game Balancing

We analyze aggregate gameplay patterns to understand how players interact with our games. This includes score distribution, difficulty balance, feature usage, crash rates, and retention.

Where practical, we use aggregated or pseudonymous data for analytics and game balancing purposes.

9. Cloud Save, Profiles, Leaderboards, and Friends

Our games may provide a persistent game profile linked to your platform account. Cloud save stores your progress, settings, match data, inventory, currency balances, and entitlement state so you can continue playing across devices.

If you use leaderboards or friends features, your profile information (such as display name and scores) may be visible to other players.

10. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple or Google through their respective app stores. We do not receive or store your full payment card numbers.

We may store records of your purchases and entitlements to provide, restore, and support your purchases.

11. Data Retention

We retain information as long as needed to provide game features, support purchases, and meet legal obligations.

Some data may be retained longer for platform requirements, security, legal compliance, tax and accounting purposes, or fraud prevention.

12. Data Deletion and Account Requests

You can request deletion of your data by visiting our Data Deletion page or by emailing support@onidev.io.

If you signed in through Apple or Google, account deletion for those platforms is handled by Apple or Google respectively.

Some records may be retained after a deletion request for purchase verification, fraud prevention, or legal compliance. Where practical, we may delete, de-identify, anonymize, or restrict data rather than retain it in identifiable form.

13. Children

Our games and apps are intended for general audiences unless a specific product states otherwise. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children who require parental consent without providing appropriate notices and controls.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us at support@onidev.io.

14. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. However, no system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

15. International Users

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own by our service providers, including Unity, Apple, Google, analytics providers, advertising networks, and crash reporting providers.

16. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page.
